Requirements

Documents & age criteria.

Please keep the following ready before starting the admission process.

📄 Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Report Card of previous class
  • Aadhaar Card — student & both parents
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Address proof (utility bill / rent agreement)

🎂 Age Criteria

  • Nursery3 – 4 years
  • LKG4 – 5 years
  • UKG5 – 6 years
  • Class 16+ years
  • Class 2 – 9As per previous class
  • Class 10Subject to seat availability
