Principal

Dr. Anita Sharma

M.Ed, Ph.D · Education Leadership

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire. At VAA Public School, every teacher is committed to sparking that fire in every child who walks through our gates."

With over 25 years of experience in school leadership, Dr. Sharma has been the guiding force behind VAA's transformation into one of Karnal's most respected institutions. She believes in a balance of academic rigour, moral grounding and joyful learning.

Under her leadership, VAA has introduced smart classrooms, expanded sports facilities and built a faculty development program that keeps our teachers at the forefront of modern pedagogy.
