Our Story

A journey that began with 60 students.

VAA Public School was founded in 2005 with a simple but powerful vision — to create a school in Karnal where children could learn in an environment that balances academic rigour with genuine warmth. What started with 60 students in a single building has today grown into one of the region's most trusted institutions.

Over two decades, we have built smart classrooms, science and computer labs, a well-stocked library and spacious sports grounds. But our greatest achievement is not our infrastructure — it is the 15,000+ students who have passed through our corridors and gone on to excel in medicine, engineering, business, sports and public service.

Today, with 1500+ students and 60+ dedicated faculty members, we continue to uphold the same founding values — discipline, curiosity, compassion and excellence.
